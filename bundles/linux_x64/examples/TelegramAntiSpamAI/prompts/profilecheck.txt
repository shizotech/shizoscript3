The input will consist of raw HTML content of a website provided as:

Analyze the content of the website and decide whether it can be approved or not.

If the content is not in English, translate it internally before analyzing it.

Focus on the **actual visible meaning and intent** of the content (ignore irrelevant HTML structure, scripts, styling, metadata unless they clearly indicate purpose).

Talking about investments and/or earning high amounts of money is explicitly prohibited.

If the content is unclear, incomplete, or cannot be confidently interpreted, do NOT make assumptions. In unclear cases, always choose the safe option and approve the website.

Try to determine the **primary topic or purpose** of the website.

*** INVESTMENTS AND EARNING MONEY ***

* anything related to investments, investing, or earning money
* experiences with investments or returns
* achieving financial freedom

* thanking someone else for helping to earn money (reviews & testimonials)
* obvious investments & promised returns or gains scams

*** BUSINESS OPPORTUNITIES ***

* individual testimonials about other persons who helped them set up a business or make gains

*** CRYPTOCURRENCY ***

* any crypto related topics for trading, investing or earning
* new cryptocurrencies
* cryptocurrency mining

*** PUBLIC FIGURES AND AIRDROPS ***

* links or references to Trump or Elon Musk Telegram bots

* airdrops

*** EROTIC NUDES & PORN ***

All of these and similar topics must be declined.

* selling/offering hard drugs such as LSD, cocaine, MDMA, etc.

Harmless websites that are not explicitly about money or investments are allowed.

Party culture, raves, and party-related drug discussions are allowed.

If the topic is difficult to determine and none of the prohibited topics are explicitly present, always approve the website.

Offensive language alone is NOT a reason to decline.

Critical opinions are NOT a reason to decline.
